The Challenge

Modern factories suffer from fragmented maintenance processes.

Zero Visibility

Managers cannot track real-time machine health rates, often leading to sudden breakdown cascades.

Fragmented Checklists

Manual paper sheets lead to incomplete checks, with no direct way to track daily tasks.

Communication Lag

Filing physical reports delays repair starts, leaving critical hardware unattended.

The Seibi Solution

A digital-first, real-time coordination dashboard.

Real-Time Metrics

Get instant updates on daily tasks, active issues, and equipment health status.

Floor Map Integration

Visual map shows where components are, check routing, and verify wiring instantly.

Connected Desk Flow

Operators report issues, request repairs, and access AI-powered manuals directly on the floor.
