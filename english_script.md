# Equipment Inspection App Presentation Script (English Speech)

## Opening
Good morning, everyone. I am Mark from the welding line. 
Today, I would like to report on the equipment inspection application ("Seibi") that our team developed and implemented.

---

## 1. Background & Challenges

Previously, all equipment inspections on our shop floor were conducted on paper. Once filled out, paper sheets were filed away or discarded, leaving no searchable digital record.

This created three main challenges:
1. **Lost History**: Repeat issues couldn't be confirmed without relying on memory.
2. **Lack of Follow-up**: There was no system to track whether reported defects were actually repaired.
3. **Operational Friction**: During repairs, workers had to flip through paper manuals or ask colleagues.

---

## 2. Solutions & Key Features

To solve these problems, we built the app around four core features:

1. **Digitalization & Log Preservation**
   Workers enter daily checks on mobile devices. Inspection and defect histories are saved digitally and can be searched anytime.

2. **Visual Layout Map**
   A visual layout map displays the physical arrangement and status of equipment (such as robots and regulators). Workers can intuitively locate machines, wiring routes, and status alerts at a glance.

3. **Real-Time Task Sharing & Management**
   When an abnormality is logged, it automatically becomes a repair task and sends instant notifications across all devices and LINE WORKS, preventing missed reports.

4. **AI Repair Support**
   Instead of flipping through paper manuals, workers can ask the AI questions in plain language. The AI reads the uploaded equipment manuals and draws from its internal knowledge base to provide direct answers, complete with exact manual page references.

---

## 3. Results & Future Outlook

We launched the app this month, and we expect to see benefits such as searchable log tracking, faster response times, and zero time wasted searching for manuals.

Moving forward, we will focus on three key initiatives:

1. **Data-Driven Predictive Maintenance**
   By analyzing daily checksheet logs (such as wire feeding hesitation or slippage), we can predict when conduit liners need replacement before a mid-weld failure occurs.

2. **Expanding AI Manual Coverage**
   We will import manuals and technical diagrams for all primary factory equipment into the AI system.

3. **Self-Improving AI Feedback Loop**
   By logging user questions and feedback on answers, the AI continuously improves its accuracy over time.

---

## Closing

This application is not just a digital replacement for paper forms—it is a tool built for our shop floor to eliminate downtime and keep our equipment running at its best.

Thank you for your attention.
